# Image slots

Every image the site uses, with the dimensions and intent needed to replace or
produce one without reverse-engineering the layout.

Art direction is fixed by the design thesis: **capiz shell windows — light
passes, identity is protected.** Layered translucent material, light travelling
*through* rather than bouncing off, and gold reserved for the seal. No flag
graphics, no shell illustrations, no sunburst stock, no stock-photo handshakes.

## In use

### `enotaryhub-hero-2027.webp` — 1536 × 1024, landscape

The home hero, and the single most important asset on the site.

Layered translucent vellum sheets lit from the right, a gold embossed rayed
seal, a gold signature line, over a dark navy textured ground. The left ~40% is
intentionally empty and dark: that is the type area, and any replacement **must
preserve it** or the slogan becomes unreadable.

The image also anchors two palette tokens — `--color-night` is sampled from its
ground and `--color-vellum` from its lit sheets. Replacing it with a
differently-toned photograph means retuning both in `app/tokens.css`.

- Rendered `fill`, `object-cover object-right`, `priority`
- `alt=""` and `aria-hidden` — decorative; the slogan beside it carries meaning
- A WebGL caustic composites over it on capable devices; on everything else the
  photograph stands alone, which is a complete experience, not a degraded one

## Generated by the application

### Open Graph and Twitter cards — 1200 × 630

`app/(frontend)/opengraph-image.tsx` and
`app/(frontend)/twitter-image.tsx` generate branded social cards at build time.

### Apple icon

`app/(frontend)/apple-icon.tsx` generates the 180 × 180 Apple touch icon. The
public brand treatment is a font-only `eNotaryHub` wordmark; no SVG logo or
browser icon is shipped for now.

`components/chrome/Wordmark.tsx` is the single temporary brand treatment. A
future icon should be derived from an approved identity, not invented in code.

## Rules for any new image

1. **No fabricated product screenshots.** The platform is pre-launch. Interface
   diagrams must be honest CSS or SVG, labelled as planned interfaces.
2. **No fabricated testimonials, client logos, or metrics.**
3. Decorative images take `alt=""` plus `aria-hidden`. Informative images take
   real alt text describing the information, not the picture.
4. Serve WebP or AVIF. Anything full-bleed should be at least 1536 px wide.
5. Keep the left third of any hero-position image dark and uncluttered.
